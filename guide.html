Organizer guide

From install to champion.

Follow the event flow below. All tournament administration happens through buttons and forms.

01

Install and run setup

Add the bot with its requested permissions, place its role above player roles, run /setup, then choose existing channels or automatic secured channel creation.

02

Create the event

Open the private controller, select Create Event, choose Solo or Duo Signup, and enter optional fees and payment methods. Nuke pricing is reserved for paid bot plans.

03

Open registration

Players enter an Activision ID. In Duo Signup they can invite a partner by DM or join the solo duo queue. Close registration and confirm required payments when ready.

04

Fill solo duo spots

If more solo entrants exist than available bracket spots, select Randomize Teams. Every eligible solo receives a claim DM; the fastest responses qualify and are randomly paired.

05

Reveal and adjust teams

Review the temporary team reveal. Players may request Lock, Respin, or Nuke, while administrators make the final decision. Approved fees update the prize pot.

06

Create the bracket

The bot generates a visual single-elimination bracket with automatic byes and opens private match rooms. BO3 uses HP / HP / SND; the Grand Final uses BO5.

07

Confirm results

One player from each side confirms the winner and score. An administrator approves advancement, and later rounds remain locked until the current round finishes.

08

Crown and close

Grand Final winners receive a temporary five-hour trophy nickname. Select Close Event to remove temporary rooms, teams, and the live bracket.
